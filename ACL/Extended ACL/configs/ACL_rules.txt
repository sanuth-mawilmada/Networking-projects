=========
Rule list
=========


Permit Internet access only for the IT department subnet. Deny all Internet traffic originating from the HR department subnet. 

Restrict Mail Server access to PC1 and PC3 in the IT department. Deny access to all other hosts.

Block all traffic between IT department subnet and HR department subnet.

Permit only PC2 from the IT Department to access the File Server. Deny access from all other internal hosts and external networks.

Only PC1 from IT department can establish VTY management sessions to R1 and R2. Deny any access attempts from all HR hosts and other unauthorized endpoints.

Deny all incoming ICMP packets targeting any servers, while ensuring operational application traffic remains unaffected.

Permit hosts in the HR Department subnet to access only HTTP/HTTPS services on the Web server. Deny HR department hosts to access all other servers and non-web protocols in the server subnet.
